[06] COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you guarantee labels won't warp like Midjourney?

We don't rely on self-serve prompt generation alone. We extract your vector dieline or packaging CAD file, map it accurately onto a 3D UV mesh, and control the scene lighting and camera coordinates before applying diffusion shaders and human typography polish.

What do you need from us to begin?

Just your 2D packaging dielines (AI, PDF, or vector) or 5 clear iPhone photos of your existing product from various angles. Our team handles 100% of the 3D twin construction.

What formats and resolutions are delivered?

All assets are delivered in 4K resolution across standard advertising ratios: 1:1 (Square Feed), 9:16 (Instagram Reels & TikTok), 4:5 (Meta Portrait), and 16:9 (Display/Web). Both raw renders and layered ad creatives are provided.

Can we request revisions?

Yes. Growth Engine subscribers receive unlimited revisions with direct communication in a private Slack channel.
